Frequently Asked Questions
How do I know which room I have been allotted?

Open Hostel → Room Details. Your block, level, room number and bed are listed there once registration is completed.

Can I change my room type after registration?

Room type is fixed for the academic year. Change requests are considered only on valid grounds, through the hostel office.

What are the available room types?

3, 4, 6 and 8 sharing A/C rooms, and 4 sharing bunker A/C rooms.

Can I register for both hostel and transport?

No. A student can register for only one facility, either Hostel or Transport.

Whom do I contact for room maintenance issues?

Raise a request with your block warden or at the hostel office.

Is Wi-Fi available in the hostel?

Yes, campus Wi-Fi is available in all hostel blocks.

Hostel Rules and Regulations
  1. Students must carry their ID card at all times and show it when asked.
  2. Entry and exit timings must be strictly followed as notified by the hostel office.
  3. Visitors are allowed only in the designated visitors area.
  4. Silence must be maintained during study hours and after lights-out.
  5. Cooking and the use of heating appliances inside rooms are not permitted.
  6. Students are responsible for the furniture and fittings in their rooms; damage will be charged.
  7. Ragging in any form is strictly prohibited and punishable.
  8. Smoking, alcohol and prohibited substances are not allowed on campus.
  9. Room changes are allowed only with written permission from the hostel office.
  10. Leaving the campus overnight requires prior permission and an entry in the outpass register.

Frequently Asked Questions
Who can register for transport?

Day scholars who have not registered for hostel accommodation.

Can I change my boarding point?

Boarding point changes are allowed once per semester through the transport office.

Will buses run on holidays?

Buses run on working days as per the academic calendar.

What should I carry while travelling?

Your university ID card and the transport pass.

Whom do I contact if I miss the bus?

Contact the transport office or the route coordinator.
